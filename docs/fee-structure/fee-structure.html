Service & Investment Structure

A transparent look at our fees.

Prepared for prospective members considering our introduction and consultancy service.

Heritage Union is a private introduction and consultancy service. This document outlines, in full transparency, how our engagement works, what is included at each stage, and how costs are structured and shared between Heritage Union and our clients.

$25,000 — Full service, delivered across two USD payments

This structure reflects our approach to the work itself: we advise, match, and introduce, but once an introduction is made, we operate on trust and do not interfere in or monitor the relationship between client and candidate.

Our Process

  1. 1

    Introductory Consultation: A complimentary, no-obligation Zoom conversation between you and Heritage Union to understand your goals and answer your questions.

  2. 2

    Concierge Initiation: Upon engagement, we begin in-depth consultations to build your profile, which you review and approve before anything moves forward.

  3. 3

    Matching: We compare your approved profile with the women who have registered themselves with Heritage Union. We do not search for, approach or recruit women on your behalf. Every candidate chose to register on her own.

  4. 4

    Profile Review: You have the opportunity to review up to 20 profiles of women who have registered with Heritage Union and agreed to their profile being shown to clients. Profiles never include contact details. Working together, we then identify the five most suitable.

  5. 5

    Introductions: You have the opportunity to be introduced to the five most suitable profiles. Before any introduction, each woman receives your background disclosure in Vietnamese and decides whether to share her contact details. For each woman who agrees, we facilitate a formal introduction using your preferred channel: phone, social media, or another method of your choosing.

  6. 6

    Organic Relationship Development: From the point of introduction, the relationship develops naturally between you and the candidate, at your own pace. We do not monitor or interfere, though we remain available to offer support if needed, particularly cultural guidance for both client and candidate.

Success Recognition — An Honour System

Heritage Union's full service is valued at $25,000 USD. The $15,000 USD Concierge Initiation fee covers our consultation, matching, and introduction work. The remaining $10,000 USD operates on an honour system: once introductions are made, we step back and do not monitor, interfere in, or otherwise intrude upon the relationship between client and candidate.

Should the relationship develop into an outcome both parties consider successful (broadly understood as a relationship in which both individuals are committed, happy, and fulfilled), we trust our clients to honour our work by completing payment of the final $10,000 USD, bringing the total investment in our full service to $25,000 USD. This payment is never monitored for or chased; it reflects the trust-based relationship we aim to build with every client.

Throughout this stage, Heritage Union remains available to offer support if requested, particularly cultural guidance and understanding for both client and candidate, but we do not monitor or involve ourselves in how the relationship unfolds.

Engagement Timeline

We expect the full process, from initiation through introductions, to conclude within 12 months. If, after 12 months, no agreement has been reached on proceeding with any of the five introductions offered, Heritage Union and the client will part ways amicably, with no further payment due from either party.
