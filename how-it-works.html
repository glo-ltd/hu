The Heritage Union Journey

Six stages. One considered path.

Nothing here is automated and nothing is rushed.

  1. 1

    A private conversation

    A free, discreet consultation with James to understand your hopes, your circumstances, and what a meaningful connection looks like for you. No obligation, no pressure.

  2. 2

    Your relationship profile

    Together, we build a considered picture of your values, your circumstances, and the qualities that matter most to you — the foundation for everything that follows. You review and approve your profile before anything moves forward.

  3. 3

    Candidate search

    Drawing on our network across Vietnam, we search among candidates who have opted in and are genuinely open to connection, identifying those whose values and circumstances align with your profile.

  4. 4

    A curated shortlist

    From our search, we review candidates and present you with the most suitable matches — never a catalogue to browse. Working together, you select those you wish to pursue for introduction.

  5. 5

    Considered introductions

    We facilitate a formal introduction between you and each of your chosen candidates, using whichever channel feels most natural — call, video, or message.

  6. 6

    Connection, at your own pace

    From the point of introduction, the relationship develops naturally, on your own timeline. We remain available to offer cultural guidance and support as things progress — but we don't monitor or intrude on how the connection unfolds. When the time is right, we can help coordinate a visit to meet in person, with practical arrangements handled discreetly.

The Heritage Union Trust Framework

Why this process works.

Every stage is held together by the same principles — a considered process, genuine local presence, founder involvement, and support that continues.

A considered process

We take the time to understand what genuinely matters to you, rather than rushing toward a match.

Genuine local presence

Our network on the ground in Vietnam means candidates are understood in real context, not assessed from a distance.

Founder involvement

James is personally involved in every consultation. This is a consultancy he leads directly, not a system that runs without him.

Cultural guidance, throughout

Huyen, our independent cultural expert and liaison, supports both sides as a connection develops — helping navigate language, distance, and expectations with confidence, from introduction onward.
